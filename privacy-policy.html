Privacy Policy

What this site collects — which is almost nothing.

Last updated 10 November 2026

HeartGuide is a small reading site. It does not run analytics, it does not show advertising, it does not have user accounts, and it does not track you. This policy says so in plain language, because privacy policies are usually where that gets buried.

What we collect

Almost nothing. Reading a guide does not ask you for a name, an email address, or anything else. The site does not set advertising or tracking cookies, and there is no analytics script watching which pages you visit.

The one exception is email. If you write to us at dannisjersey@gmail.com, we will have your email address and whatever you wrote — because that is how email works. We use it only to read your message and reply. We do not sell it, share it, or add it to any list.

Cookies

The site uses one cookie, and only to remember the choice you made on the cookie banner (accept or decline). It is stored in your own browser, it contains no personal information, and reading the guides works the same either way.

Third parties

The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page loads. Google may log that request — including your IP address — under its own privacy policy. There are no other third-party scripts on the site. The stock videos on the site are hosted here, on this site, not streamed from a third party.

Adults only

HeartGuide is written for adults. It is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, this site is not for you.

Changes to this policy

If the site ever adds analytics, advertising, or anything else that collects data, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be noted here with a new date. We will not quietly start collecting things.

Contact and governing law

Questions about this policy: dannisjersey@gmail.com. This policy and the site are governed by the laws of Pakistan.
