How the guides are written

Written to be used, not just read

“Most dating advice tells you what to be. We'd rather tell you what to do — Tuesday, 7pm, coffee in hand.”— Jordan Ellis

Steps in order

Exactly what to do

If a guide says “text them back”, it tells you what to write, when to send it, and what to do next — not “be confident”.

Honest limits

When to get help

When a problem needs a professional — a counsellor, a therapist — we say that too, instead of pretending every problem has a DIY answer.

Corrections welcome

Kept up to date

Dating apps change, norms change, people change. Readers' corrections are how guides stay accurate — tell us through the Contact page.
