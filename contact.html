Contact

Get in touch

Corrections, suggestions, or a guide you wish existed — write to us.

Email us at dannisjersey@gmail.com

There is no contact form on this site — email is the fastest way to reach a real person, and it keeps things simple.

What to write about

  • A correction. If a step in a guide is out of date, unclear, or just wrong, tell us which guide and what happened. That is the most useful email you can send.
  • A suggestion. If a dating problem you are facing is not covered here, describe it in a line or two. Suggestions shape what gets written next.
  • A question. If a guide left you unsure about your own situation, ask. We cannot give personal advice to everyone, but we do read every message.

When you will hear back

We read everything. Replies usually take a few days — this is a small site, run by one person, and the guides come first. If your message is a correction, it may show up as a fix on the site before you get a reply, which is the point.

Please do not send personal details you would not want shared, and do not send anything you would not want a stranger to read. Email is not a private counselling service — if you need professional help, a counsellor or therapist is the right call.
